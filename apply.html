About the position

Pristine Kosher is one of the largest kosher kitchen kashering companies in North America. During the three weeks leading up to Pesach, our teams help families prepare their kitchens. This is a fast-paced, physically active role that requires professionalism, punctuality, and excellent customer service. You’ll be working inside customers’ homes and are expected to represent Pristine Kosher professionally at all times.

Compensation

  • Kitchen work: $30.00/hour
  • Driving time between jobs: $25.00/hour (tracked separately)
  • Gas and tolls are reimbursed
  • Workers log their hours every day
